Observed failures
=================

Three build-11 attempts stopped at the Finder layout gate before DMG signing
and before Apple submission:

- releases/SkyPC-0.9.38-11-20260924T154200Z.D9RWeS
- releases/SkyPC-0.9.38-11-20260924T154325Z.goSgPk
- releases/SkyPC-0.9.38-11-20260924T154536Z.26me8n

Finder returned -10006 while setting the position of SkyPC.app. A bounded
retry did not fix the failure. A read-only diagnostic against a mounted copy
showed that Finder's front window was the unrelated home window named
"khoakheu", while the exact mounted folder already exposed Applications and
SkyPC.app. The previous script captured front window immediately after an
asynchronous open and therefore addressed the wrong container.

Fix
===

finder_layout.applescript now obtains targetFolder from the supplied mount
alias and addresses `container window of targetFolder`. It retains a bounded,
idempotent retry for item population. It no longer depends on Finder focus or
the user's other open Finder windows.
