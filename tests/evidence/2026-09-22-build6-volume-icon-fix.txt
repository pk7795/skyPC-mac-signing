Diagnosis
- Read-only inspection of both `build/SkyPC-rw.dmg` and

- The contained app icon remains byte-identical to the source ICNS.
- The final candidate still passes the Finder background bookmark, relative
  alias/CNID, 900x351 logical size, window and icon-position verifier.
- The script had applied and checked the volume icon before asking Finder to
  persist the background/layout. Finder's update removed the file/flag, and the
  script did not check them again until after signing, notarization and staple.
- Exact read-only evidence:
  - tests/evidence/2026-09-22-build6-candidate-icon-diagnosis.txt
  - tests/evidence/2026-09-22-build6-rw-icon-diagnosis.txt

Fix
- `release_skypc.sh` now lets Finder write and verifies `.DS_Store` first, then
  copies `.VolumeIcon.icns`, applies hidden/custom-icon flags, and calls the full
  volume check immediately before sync/detach.
- The service contract double now simulates Finder deleting an earlier volume
  icon. The accepted-flow test requires `osascript` to precede `SetFile`.
- The signed build-6 candidate was not modified. Adding the missing icon would
  change its contents and invalidate its existing signature/ticket.
